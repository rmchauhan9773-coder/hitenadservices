Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Growth Hardik (“we”, “our” or “us”) may handle information when you visit this website.

Information you provide

If you contact us by email, we may receive your name, email address and the information you include in your message. We use it to respond to your enquiry and maintain relevant correspondence. The contact form on this static website opens your email application; it does not itself submit or store data on our server.

Automatically collected information

Our hosting provider and website infrastructure may process technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, requested pages and access time for security, delivery and troubleshooting. The exact data collected depends on the hosting provider and services enabled.

Cookies and advertising

This website may use cookies or similar technologies if analytics or advertising services are enabled. If Google AdSense is used, Google and its partners may use cookies or other technologies to serve, measure and personalise ads as permitted by applicable settings, consent requirements and policies. You can review Google’s advertising and privacy information through Google’s own privacy resources.

Analytics and third-party services

If analytics, embedded media, fonts, forms or other third-party services are added, those providers may collect information under their own privacy policies. We will update this page to describe material services actually used on this website.

How we use information

  • To respond to messages and requests.
  • To protect, maintain and improve the website.
  • To understand website performance where analytics are enabled.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations.

Data retention and security

We retain correspondence for as long as reasonably needed for the purpose for which it was received or as required by law. We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices

You may contact us to ask about personal information you have sent to us. You can also manage cookies through your browser settings. Some website features may not work as intended if cookies are disabled.

Children’s privacy

This website is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review the request.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the website or its services change. The updated date will be shown at the top of this page.

Contact
