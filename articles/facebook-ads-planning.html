How to Plan a Facebook Ads Campaign

Educational article · Updated 7 November 2026

← Back to all guides

A Facebook or Instagram advertising campaign should begin with a business objective and a clear understanding of the audience. The campaign setup is only one part of the work: the offer, creative, destination and follow-up process all influence results.

1. Define one objective

Decide what action matters: awareness, website visits, enquiries or another supported outcome. Choose the campaign objective that most closely matches the action you want people to take.

2. Prepare the offer and destination

Explain what is being offered, who it is for and what the next step involves. If the ad sends people to a website, make sure the landing page answers the same question as the ad and works well on mobile.

3. Create clear advertising material

Use a relevant image or video, readable text and a direct call to action. Avoid exaggerated claims, misleading before-and-after comparisons and statements that imply knowledge of a viewer’s personal attributes. Review the platform’s current advertising policies before publishing.

4. Choose an audience thoughtfully

Use appropriate location, age and other permitted settings based on the product, service and applicable law. Avoid unnecessary restrictions. Audience tools and targeting options change, so check the current Ads Manager interface.

5. Set a controlled budget

Choose a budget you can afford to test. Decide how long the test will run and what evidence you need before making changes. Do not assume a small number of impressions or clicks predicts long-term performance.

6. Measure meaningful results

Review delivery, reach, impressions, clicks, cost per click and the action that matters to the business. For lead campaigns, assess whether enquiries are relevant and can be contacted, not only the cost per form submission.

7. Improve methodically

Change one major variable at a time when possible. Keep notes about creative, audience, budget and dates so comparisons are meaningful. Follow applicable privacy, consent and data-handling requirements when collecting leads.

Conclusion

A well-planned campaign connects a clear objective with a relevant offer, useful creative, an appropriate destination and honest measurement. No platform or agency can guarantee a specific result.

This article is general educational information. Check current platform documentation and policies before making business or advertising decisions.
