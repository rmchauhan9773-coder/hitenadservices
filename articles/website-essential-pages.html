Essential Pages Every Business Website Needs

Educational article · Updated 7 November 2026

← Back to all guides

A business website should help visitors quickly understand what the organisation does, who it serves and how to take the next step. The exact pages depend on the type of website, but several core pages are useful for many organisations.

Home page

Explain the website’s purpose in plain language. Give visitors clear routes to the most important topics, products or services. Avoid vague claims and cluttered navigation.

About page

Describe the organisation, its purpose, relevant experience and how its content or services are created. Be accurate and do not invent qualifications, customer numbers or testimonials.

Services or information page

Explain each service or topic, what is included and any important limitations. If prices or delivery details vary, explain how visitors can request accurate information.

Contact page

Provide a working contact method. A form should clearly explain what happens after submission and should collect only information that is needed. Test it on desktop and mobile.

Privacy policy

Explain what information is collected, why it is used, how third-party services may process it and how visitors can contact the website about privacy. Keep this page consistent with the tools actually installed on the website.

Disclaimer and terms

A disclaimer can explain the limits of informational content and third-party links. Terms can describe acceptable use and ownership. These pages should reflect the website’s actual operations and applicable law.

Helpful navigation and maintenance

Link important pages in the header or footer. Remove template text, broken links and unfinished sections. Review contact details and policies whenever the website’s tools or services change.

Conclusion

Core pages support clarity and trust, but simply publishing policy pages does not make a website high quality. The most important part is providing genuinely useful information and maintaining it over time.
