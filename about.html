About Growth Hardik

A learning-focused website sharing practical knowledge about digital marketing and websites.

Our purpose

Growth Hardik is an independent educational website created to make digital marketing concepts easier to understand. We publish guides about social media marketing, paid advertising, search engine optimisation, website creation and related topics.

Who the content is for

Our resources are intended for beginners, students, freelancers, creators and small business owners who want to understand digital tools and make more informed decisions.

How we create content

We aim to explain topics in original language, use practical examples where helpful, distinguish general education from professional advice, and review articles when relevant tools or processes change. Readers should independently verify platform-specific settings and policies before acting.

Editorial principles

  • Clarity: explain terminology in plain language.
  • Usefulness: focus on actionable information rather than filler.
  • Transparency: identify limitations and avoid unrealistic guarantees.
  • Maintenance: improve pages when information becomes outdated.
